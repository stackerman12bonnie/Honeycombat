## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**HoneyCombat** is an engaging strategy game for Windows that puts you in the role of a bee commander tasked with defending your queen against enemy bees. The latest version offers a unique twist on traditional tower defense mechanics, allowing you to float above your troops and enhance them with drops of colored honey. Each honey color provides distinct upgrades for your bee army, creating a strategic layer to the gameplay. With three difficulty levels—easy, normal, and hard—HoneyCombat caters to various player preferences and skill levels.
